The cadence

Twenty-nine routines, each with a frequency and an owner area.

Setup builds your cadence from your answers: your team, your tech stack, your company values. These are the routines it starts you with. Edit them freely; the skills read whatever is there.

Get started

From clone to your first planned week.

Clone and run setup

Answer a few questions about you, your manager, your direct reports and the tools your team uses. Python 3.8 or later, nothing to install.

python3 setup_management_os.py

Get your workspace

A git repository of markdown notes and HTML reports, already filled in with your people, stakeholders and initiatives, plus your personal cadence.

Open it in Claude Code

Setup offers to continue there: connect your tools, pick a report design, and plan your first week.

Claude Code skills

Ask in plain words. The skills do the bookkeeping.

Every workspace ships with seven skills. They read your cadence and notes, and write back to plain files you can open in any editor.

plan-week

plan my week

Builds the week's journal from your cadence: daily tasks for each working day, weekly tasks with one 1:1 per report, monthly and quarterly items you haven't done yet, and anything left over from last week. Suggests a focus that serves the month's intentions.

writes my_performance/weeks/2026-W43.md
morning-pulse

morning pulse

A two-minute briefing, blockers first: today's calendar with 1:1 prep, Jira issues that are stuck, PRs from your reports, Slack and email waiting on you, and today's tasks.

writes a short summary under today's notes
sprint-goalsEvery sprint

sprint check-in

Records the goals and owners your team sets at sprint planning. Halfway through, the check-in catches goals that are off track, already achieved or need realigning, and records what the team decided. At the end, the final check records what was accomplished. Then it builds the sprint report for stakeholders: what the team achieved, and what it focuses on next sprint.

writes team_performance/sprints/2026-10-12-sprint-42.md · sprint report
connect-tools

connect my tools

Connects Jira, Slack, Gmail, Google Calendar and GitHub one at a time. You pick the board, channels and repos from lists, confirm each report's accounts, and every connection gets a test read.

writes .management_os.json (IDs only, no secrets)
report-templates

set up my report design

Choose one of five design presets or bring your own colours, fonts and logo. The design applies to every report, and new templates come from a catalogue of twelve common manager reports.

writes reports/my_report_design.html
team-metrics

team metrics for last sprint

Collects each direct report's activity for a sprint, month or quarter: tickets created, closed and in progress from Jira, and commits and PRs created, merged, reviewed and commented on from GitHub. Compares each person with their own earlier periods, never with each other, and asks what the numbers don't show before you use them.

writes team_performance/metrics/2026-09-01_2026-09-30.md
first-90-daysNew in the role

plan my first 90 days

Builds your own 30/60/90-day plan from the situation you're walking into, with the conversations to have with your boss, a listening tour of your team and stakeholders, early wins and team assessment. Prepares each first meeting, records what you learn in each person's notes, and runs checkpoint reviews at days 30, 60 and 90. Based on the ideas in The First 90 Days by Michael D. Watkins.

writes my_performance/first-90-days/plan.md

Reports

Reports people actually read, ready to fill in.

Self-contained HTML in your colours, fonts and logo. Open one in a browser, click any text to edit it, then print to PDF.

Need something else? The report-templates skill adds any of eight more from its catalogue, from a 1:1 summary to a promotion case, in the same design.

Morning pulse

Unblock before anything else.

The pulse ranks what needs you by who it's blocking, across every connected tool. It only reads. Replies, comments and status changes stay with you.

Yours, on disk

Markdown and self-contained HTML in a git repository you own. No server, no database, no account to create. Open a report in a browser, edit it, print to PDF.

Read-only by design

The skills read Jira, Slack, email, your calendar and GitHub. They never send a message, post a comment or move a ticket.

No secrets in the workspace

You sign in to each tool in your browser. The workspace only stores IDs and settings, like which board and which channels.

Made for real teams

Notes about people deserve care. Keep your workspace in a private repository and connect only the tools your company allows.

Requires

Python 3.8 or later. Claude Code for the skills. Git if you want version history.

Connects to

Jira Cloud, Slack, Gmail and Google Calendar, GitHub.

Not yet
