People Delivery Stakeholder Strategy
Daily
Morning team pulse
Scan Slack/standups for blockers. Respond within 30 min. Unblock before anything else.
Delivery
Protect deep work time
Block 1–2 hrs for non-meeting work. Guard it. Cancel it last.
Strategy
End-of-day wrap
Note one win, one risk in this week's journal. Takes 3 minutes.
Delivery
Intentional visibility
One proactive message to a stakeholder or peer per day. No ask — just signal.
Stakeholder
Read team PRs
Stay in the review queue passively. Read the team's PRs. No comment needed — just keep the mental model current.
Strategy
Email triage
Scan inbox once per day. Action work items and approvals. Archive noise. Newsletters go to a Reading label — not the inbox.
Delivery
Weekly
1:1s with direct reports
They own the agenda. Ask: what's draining you? What's unclear? What do you need from me?
People
Sprint health check
Review velocity, carry-over, and scope creep before the team meeting. Log it in team_performance/sprints.md.
Delivery
Weekly status update
Send a 5-line update to key stakeholders: done, in progress, risks, needs.
Stakeholder
Team recognition
Call out one person publicly. Specific, recent, timely. Non-optional.
People
Friday priorities review
30 min on Fridays. What did you intend vs. what happened? Adjust next week's plan.
Strategy
Clear open loops
Triage unread threads, pending decisions, open PRs needing your input.
Delivery
Technical reading block
45 min. Alternate tracks: team (a recent decision, ADR, or pattern) and your tech ecosystem (one topic on rotation).
Strategy
Managing up
Pre-brief your manager before key meetings — message the day before with the outcome you want and what you need from them. Share significant wins same-day.
Stakeholder
Monthly
Career conversations
Dedicated 30-min session per report — separate from the regular 1:1. Focus: energy, growth direction, what's next. Not tasks. Don't batch everyone in the same week.
People
Roadmap review
Is the team building the right thing? Check alignment with product and stakeholders. Re-prioritise if needed.
Delivery
Stakeholder sync
Proactive 30-min with your key stakeholder. Don't wait to be asked. Set the agenda yourself.
Stakeholder
Hiring pipeline check
Even if not actively hiring: review team gaps, update JDs, maintain relationships with candidates.
People
Retro with yourself
What process is slowing the team down? What debt is accumulating? Pick one thing to fix.
Strategy
Technical learning pair
30–45 min with a senior engineer (rotate across team). Ask them to walk through a recent decision or piece of code. Learning posture, not review.
Strategy
Quarterly
Performance reviews
Structured feedback for each report. Evidence-based. Tied to growth goals set together.
People
OKR / goal setting
Co-create team goals with the team. Bottom-up input, then align with company direction.
Strategy
Team health survey
Anonymous pulse: psychological safety, clarity, workload, growth. Act on results visibly.
People
Tech strategy review
Assess tech debt backlog, platform risks, and capability gaps. Go into one part of the codebase you don't fully understand. Write a one-pager.
Strategy
Stakeholder calibration
Are your stakeholders' expectations aligned with your team's capacity and direction?
Stakeholder
Your own development
What skill are you building this quarter? Book at least one external event, book, or mentor conversation.
Strategy
Technical mini-project
Build one small tool or script solo (4–6 hrs total). Tooling over product code. Log what you built and what you learned.
Strategy
Topic rotation
Pick one topic from your tech ecosystem to go deep on this quarter. Document the key takeaway.
Strategy
Team working agreement
Co-create with the team: how you communicate, how blockers get raised, what good looks like. Team shapes it — not the manager. Revisit every 6 months or after major team changes.
