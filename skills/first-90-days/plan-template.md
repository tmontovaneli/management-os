- [ ] First early win chosen

### Days 31–60 · Decide
- [ ] Hypotheses from the listening tour tested; themes written up

- [ ] Alignment check (strategy, structure, systems, skills)
- [ ] First early win delivered; second one under way
- [ ] Checkpoint on day 60

- [ ] Early wins delivered and made visible
- [ ] Team and alignment changes agreed and started
- [ ] Plan for the rest of the year agreed with my boss
- [ ] Checkpoint on day 90: final review and what's next
